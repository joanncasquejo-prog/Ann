Legal

Privacy Notice

BPO Readiness processes personal data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Full name, email address, and Philippine mobile number
  • Payment confirmation details from our payment provider
  • Your booking history and class attendance
  • Your post-class survey answers

What we don't collect

Classes are not recorded, and we don't keep your voice or video. We don't collect government IDs.

How we use it

  • To book your seat and send your receipt, reminders and class link
  • To give the class leader the roster so only booked students are admitted
  • To process refunds and reschedules
  • To check class quality and how well each BPO Experience Leader teaches

Who sees it

The BPO Experience Leader for your class sees your name on the roster. Leaders see survey results only as combined averages, never your individual answers. Our payment, email and SMS providers process your data only to deliver their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as tax rules require. Other personal data is deleted or anonymised 12 months after your last class.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or object to its processing, by contacting our Data Protection Officer.
