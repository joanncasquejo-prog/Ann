The class

Three hours, the way BPO hiring runs

Each hour follows a stage of the real hiring process. You speak in every hour; this isn't a lecture.

  1. 1

    How hiring works, screening and English

    Hour 1

    What happens at each step of BPO hiring. Practise your one-minute introduction, then pronunciation and the grammar mistakes that cost applicants in English assessments.

    Your introduction, corrected by your leader

  2. 2

    HR and behavioural answers

    Hour 2

    How to answer "tell me about a time…" questions with situation, action and result. Practise in pairs, then share with the class.

    Three answers you can reuse in real interviews

  3. 3

    Mock call and final interview

    Hour 3

    Your leader plays an upset customer: a billing dispute, an irate caller, or a tech problem. Every student takes a live mock call, then answers account-specific final-interview questions.

    Live feedback on empathy, call control and resolution

    Class policies

    A seat is for one named person.

    These rules keep classes small and fair for everyone who paid.

    • Your seat, your name. Each seat is booked for one person. Your leader checks names at the start of class.
    • Personal class link. Your Zoom link is emailed 24 hours before class. Don't share it; only booked names are admitted.
    • Join on time. The waiting room closes 15 minutes after the start time.
    • Camera and mic on. You speak in every hour, so you need a working microphone and a quiet place.
    • No recording. The class isn't recorded, and students may not record it.
    • 2-minute survey after class. It's required, and it unlocks your class notes and certificate of attendance. Your leader sees only combined results.
    • No-shows. Missing the class without rescheduling forfeits the seat with no refund.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Who leads the class?

    A BPO Experience Leader: someone with at least 2 years leading agents in a BPO as a team leader, supervisor, QA lead or trainer. The leader for each class is shown on the schedule.

    What do I need for the class?

    A phone or laptop with Zoom, a working microphone, a stable connection, and a quiet place for 3 hours. A headset helps.

    Will this class get me hired?

    No class can promise that. It prepares you for each stage of BPO hiring and gives you practice and feedback, but hiring decisions are made by employers.

    What if the class doesn't fill up?

    A class needs at least 5 students. If it doesn't reach that 48 hours before, we'll offer you a full refund or a free move to another class.

    Can I pay over the counter?

    Yes. We hold your seat for 24 hours while you pay at 7-Eleven or Cebuana. If payment isn't confirmed in that time, the seat is released.

    What if I'm charged twice?

    We detect duplicate payments by order number and refund the second charge within 5 business days.
