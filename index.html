How it works

Five stages, the way real hiring runs

Practise one stage at a time or do a full run of all five. Speaking stages use voice. Everything else runs in text, and voice stages switch to text on a weak connection.

  1. 1

    Initial screening

    Voice · ~5 min

    Questions about your background, schedule flexibility, and why you want BPO work.

    Fluency and clarity score

  2. 2

    English assessment

    Voice + text

    Read-aloud and sentence repeat by voice. Grammar and listening answers in text.

    Score per skill

  3. 3

    HR / behavioural interview

    Text

    Situational questions, with follow-ups when an answer is vague.

    Rubric score + a stronger sample answer

  4. 4

    Mock call

    Voice

    The AI plays the customer: billing disputes, irate callers, tech troubleshooting. Personas and difficulty change each time.

    Empathy, call control and resolution scores

  5. 5

    Final / account interview

    Voice

    Account-specific questions for voice, non-voice, healthcare, and tech support roles, including spoken versions of your stage 3 answers.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Is a human reviewing my interviews?

    No. The AI runs every mock interview from the first question to the feedback. No human interviewer or reviewer takes part in practice sessions.

    Does a high readiness score mean I'll get hired?

    No. A readiness score measures your practice performance. It is not a job offer or a hiring guarantee.

    What happens when my 15 days end?

    We remind you 3 days before, 1 day before, and on the day it ends. Renewing keeps your account and devices with no re-verification. If you don't renew, your progress and reports are kept for 12 months so you can pick up where you left off.

    Do you collect my face or voice to identify me?

    No. We don't use face or voice data to identify anyone. Practice recordings are used only for your feedback reports and are deleted with your account data.

    I changed phones or I'm travelling. Will I be locked out?

    You may be asked to confirm a one-time code sent to your registered number. If your account is locked, contact support. We aim to resolve appeals within 2 business days.

    What if I'm charged twice?

    We detect duplicate payments by order ID and refund the second charge within 5 business days.
