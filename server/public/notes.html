1. Your one-minute introduction

Name and where you're from → your strongest skill with a short example → why BPO and why this company → your availability for shifts.

2. Situation, action, result

Situation: one sentence of context. Action: what you did, using "I". Result: what changed, with a number if you can.

3. Mock-call checklist

  • Greet and give your name.
  • Acknowledge the feeling before the problem: "I understand how frustrating this is."
  • Confirm the details back to the customer.
  • Explain the next step and how long it takes.
  • Close: ask if there's anything else, then thank them.
