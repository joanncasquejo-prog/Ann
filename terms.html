Terms of Service

1. The Solo Pass

The BPO Readiness Solo Pass costs PHP 599 and gives access for 15 days from activation. It is renewable. A pass is activated only after our payment provider confirms your payment.

2. One named person

Your account is licensed to one named individual and is not transferable. Sharing, selling, or lending your login credentials is prohibited.

3. Devices and sessions

You may register up to 2 devices and swap devices up to 2 times per 15-day pass. Additional swaps need support review. Only one session may be active at a time; starting a new session ends any existing one.

4. Identity verification

BPO Readiness may verify your identity at any time, including by one-time codes sent to your registered mobile number, may limit devices and sessions, and may suspend accounts that show signs of sharing.

5. What "unlimited" means

"Unlimited" means unlimited mock interviews for the 15-day access period, for personal use by the account holder, one interview at a time.

6. AI practice interviewer

All mock interviews are run by an AI practice interviewer, not a real recruiter. A readiness score is not a job offer or a hiring guarantee.

7. Enforcement and appeals

Suspected sharing is handled in steps: an in-app notice, then required re-verification, then an account lock. No account is terminated on an automated flag alone; a person reviews it first. You may appeal a lock with a valid government ID, and we aim to respond within 2 business days. You may appeal a termination once, to a second reviewer.

8. Termination for sharing

If sharing is confirmed after review, the pass is cancelled and unused access is forfeited without refund. The mobile number and email on the account cannot be used for new sign-ups for 12 months.

9. Refunds

  • Full refund within 3 days of activation if you have completed 3 or fewer mock interviews.
  • After that, refunds apply only to platform outages longer than 48 hours.
  • Duplicate payments are refunded within 5 business days.
  • If the AI interviewer is unavailable for more than 4 hours in a row, your pass is extended by one day.

10. Chargebacks

An account is suspended while a chargeback is open and restored if the dispute is withdrawn or resolved in your favour.

11. Expiry

When a pass expires, content is locked. Your progress and reports are kept for 12 months, then deleted or anonymised.
