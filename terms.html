Terms of Service

1. The class

A BPO Readiness Live Class is a 3-hour live online class on Zoom led by a BPO Experience Leader. Each seat costs PHP 599 and covers one class. Each class has a maximum of 10 students.

2. One named person

A seat is booked for one named person and is not transferable. Your class link is personal. Only booked names are admitted from the waiting room, and sharing, selling or lending a seat or link is prohibited.

3. Booking and payment

A seat is confirmed only after our payment provider confirms your payment. Over-the-counter payments hold a seat for 24 hours; if payment isn't confirmed in that time, the seat is released.

4. Rescheduling

You may move to another class once, free of charge, up to 24 hours before your class starts, subject to seat availability.

5. Cancellations and refunds

  • Full refund if you cancel at least 48 hours before the class starts.
  • No refund for cancellations less than 48 hours before the class, or for no-shows.
  • Duplicate payments are refunded within 5 business days.

6. If we cancel a class

A class runs only if at least 5 students are booked 48 hours before it starts. If a class doesn't reach that number, or the leader can't run it, you may choose a full refund or a free move to another class.

7. Attendance

The waiting room closes 15 minutes after the start time. Students need a working microphone and are expected to take part in speaking activities, including a mock customer call. The leader may remove anyone who disrupts the class, without refund.

8. No recording

Classes are not recorded. Students may not record, screenshot or stream the class.

9. No job guarantee

The class prepares you for BPO hiring. It is not a job offer or a hiring guarantee, and hiring decisions are made by employers.

10. Chargebacks

If you file a chargeback, your booking is suspended until the dispute is resolved.
